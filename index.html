The method

Gain strength.
Move well.

Your sport training comes first. Every session is planned around your skills work, sparring and fight dates, so you get stronger without losing sharpness.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We test movement, strength, power and conditioning, then go through your training week and injury history. That shows us exactly where you're losing out.

  2. 02

    Build

    Progressive strength work that carries over to your sport: force through the floor, a stronger trunk, and greater capacity in injury-prone areas.

  3. 03

    Move

    Mobility, speed and elastic power, so the strength shows up in how you punch, sprawl, tackle and change direction.

  4. 04

    Peak

    Conditioning and load planned backwards from fight or match day. You arrive fresh, powerful and able to go the distance.

Your coach

Tommy Woodward

I'm a sports performance coach specialising in combat and contact athletes. I came to coaching from rugby. Post-concussion syndrome ended my playing career, and that taught me how much durability matters and what it takes to train around a demanding contact sport.

Now I use that experience to help other athletes get stronger, stay robust and stay in the game for longer.

My approach is simple. Get strong in the movements that matter, move well enough to use that strength, and plan everything around your sport. No wasted sessions and no generic templates.

FAQ

Questions

Will lifting make me slow or bulky?

No. Well-planned strength training makes athletes faster and more powerful. We manage training volume and nutrition so you stay in your weight class.

I already train my sport most days. How does this fit in?

Your sport comes first. We plan S&C around your skills and sparring sessions, usually two or three sessions a week, and reduce the load in fight week.

Do I need lifting experience?

No. Everyone starts with an assessment, and beginners learn the key lifts properly before we add heavy weight.

How does online coaching work?

Your programme is delivered to an app. You log sessions and send videos of key lifts, and I review everything and update the plan each week.

Do you work with non-combat athletes?

Yes. Rugby, football and other contact or field sports have very similar needs, and I'm happy to discuss any sport.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how you train with me and how often. Book a free 15-minute consultation and I'll recommend the right option and give you prices.
